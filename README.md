## 🧠 Overview

This project is a real-time inventory intelligence dashboard designed to help retail managers and businesses tackle the twin challenges of **understocking** and **overstocking**. By combining interactive heatmaps, alert systems, and product-level analytics, it ensures that inventory decisions are data-driven, timely, and localized.

Our platform empowers supply chain teams to visualize demand patterns and make informed stocking decisions — minimizing wastage, preventing missed sales, and improving customer satisfaction.

---

## 💡 Core Objective

**Reduce Inventory Loss & Maximize Availability.**  
Through demand prediction and anomaly detection, this dashboard addresses key retail pain points:

- Overstock → excess holding costs, wastage, markdowns  
- Understock → lost sales, poor customer experience  

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- **📦 Product Catalog**  
  Displays real-time demand classification for each product (High / Medium / Low)

- **📊 Understock & Overstock Metrics**  
  Highlights understock rate, wastage trends, and inventory gaps with visual cues

- **🔥 Heatmaps for Demand Forecasting**  
  Time-based demand data across days/hours, helping identify trends and high-pressure periods

- **⚙️ Smart Alerts**  
  Triggers notifications for low inventory, forecasted spikes, and high wastage rates

- **🧠 Inventory Insights Panel**  
  Each product card includes current stock, stock health, replenishment needs

- **🌤️ Future-Proof Design**  
  Mock UI components for integrating weather and social sentiment to enhance accuracy

- **💾 Optimized Performance**  
  Sub-50ms response time ensures seamless navigation and visualization rendering
